From the first ring to a booked appointment

Follow one call through Fillo, step by step. This is what happens every time a customer calls your business.

01

A customer calls. Fillo picks up instantly.

No hold music, no voicemail and no missed calls. Fillo answers on the first ring, day or night, and greets every caller in a natural, friendly voice.

  • Answers on the first ring, 24/7
  • Greets the caller naturally
  • No queues, even at busy times

Questions about Fillo

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Fillo is the AI form filling and lead processing agent built by Telza AI.

It takes the leads you already collect and handles repetitive tasks such as filling forms, submitting records, checking supported TCPA and DNC data, retrying failed submissions, and updating connected workflows.

No.

Fillo works with leads you already have. Its job is to automate the work that happens after the lead enters your system.

Yes. Fillo can take lead information from your workflow and enter it into configured web forms automatically.

Your team does not need to type the same information for every lead.

Fillo can retry failed submissions automatically.

The job is logged so your team can see what happened and whether the retry was successful. If something still needs attention, it can be flagged for review.

Fillo can log and check configured submissions after they are sent. If a submission fails, Fillo can retry the job and show the result to your team. This helps reduce the chance of failed submissions going unnoticed.

Fillo can run supported TCPA checks automatically as part of the lead processing workflow. The result can then be used to decide whether the lead continues to the next step or needs to be flagged for review.
